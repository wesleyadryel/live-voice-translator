# Privacy

Live Voice Translator does not operate its own backend and does not include analytics.

- Your OpenAI API key is stored in `chrome.storage.local` in your Chrome profile.
- Audio from the selected tab and microphone is sent directly to OpenAI only while a session is active.
- Meeting summaries and, if enabled, transcripts are stored locally in Chrome on this device.
- You choose the local retention period in the extension settings; clearing history removes these local records.

Use a dedicated OpenAI project key with a spend limit. Before recording or transcribing other people, obtain any notice or consent required by the laws and policies that apply to your meeting.
